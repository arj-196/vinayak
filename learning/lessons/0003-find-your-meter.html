What you're spending, and against what

Everything you send and everything Claude sends back is measured in tokens. You will never count them and you do not need to. It is simply the unit the meter uses.

On Pro, two limits run at the same time — and people who get caught out are almost always watching the wrong one. ¹

Clock one · session

Resets every 5 hours

The one that stops you mid-job. Short, and it refills soon — being blocked here means waiting, not being out for the week.

Clock two · weekly

Fixed day and time

Applies across all models, and resets at a time assigned to your account — the same slot every week, whenever you started. This is the one worth knowing by heart.

The unit of text Claude reads and writes — very roughly three-quarters of a word. "Tokens" is just the meter's currency. Nobody counts them by hand; you read the percentage.

One thing I will not tell you

Anthropic's documentation does not say exactly when a five-hour session begins — whether it starts at your first message, and whether sessions overlap. I could give you a confident-sounding rule, but I would be guessing.

So don't learn a rule. Read the countdown. Your usage screen shows the time left in the current session, which is the real answer and is always right.

Take a baseline

Why today specifically

You are about to start using Cowork, which will change these numbers dramatically. A "before" reading is only available before — take it next month and it tells you nothing. Five minutes now buys you a comparison you cannot reconstruct later.

  1. Open the usage screen

    In the desktop app: Settings → Usage. ⁴ If you have never opened this, that is the normal case — most people never do until something stops working.

  2. Find the weekly reset

    Somewhere on that screen is the day and time your weekly allowance refills. This is the single most useful fact on the page. It is fixed — the same slot every week — so once you know it, you know it forever.

    Practical version: if your reset is Tuesday morning, a big Cowork job on Monday night is the worst possible timing, and Tuesday afternoon is the best.

  3. Record what you see

    Type the four numbers in below and save. It stays in this browser, and B2 shows it back to you next to your post-Cowork reading — which is the whole point.

    Blanks are fine — record whatever your screen actually shows. Screens change; don't hunt for a field that isn't there.

  4. Watch it move

    Go and have a normal chat with Claude — five or ten messages, whatever you were going to do anyway. Then come back to Settings → Usage and look again.

    The number to notice is how little it moved. Ordinary chat barely touches a Pro allowance. That is exactly why the jump when you first run Cowork is startling — and why you are taking this reading now rather than after.

Three from memory

From memory. If you get one wrong, that's the one worth knowing — it costs nothing here and something real later.

On Pro, which two clocks are running?

A session limit resetting every five hours, and a weekly limit across all models resetting at a fixed time assigned to your account. Being stopped by the first means waiting a few hours; the second is the one worth planning around.

Which of these eats your allowance fastest?

Cowork, by a wide margin. It is agentic multi-step work executing on your behalf rather than a single reply, and Anthropic says plainly that it consumes more of your allocation than chatting. Worth knowing before your first run, not after.

Where does your weekly reset time live?

Settings → Usage — the same screen as the session countdown. Billing shows what you pay, which is a different question from what you have left.
