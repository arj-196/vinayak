# Mission shift: the goal is transferable processes, not personal fluency

Offered four preset win conditions, Vinayak rejected all of them and wrote his own:

> "Able to communicate to all models, develop master processes which can be transferred and
> implemented where the code can work."

This is a materially different goal from the provisional mission, which assumed the win was
personal efficiency — "stop re-explaining yourself". His actual target is **process
standardisation and handover**: definitions that leave his head, move to other people, and run.
The phrase "master processes" is his, and is worth adopting as the workspace's own term for what
a Skill is *for*.

**What this changed.** The original 15-lesson journey had no lesson on giving a Skill to another
person — it optimised for him using his own Skills well. That would have missed the mission
entirely. Sharing is now L04 and is treated as the centre of Stage 01, not an afterthought.
Emphasis across Stage 01 also moves from "a Skill saves you typing" to "a Skill is an artifact
that outlives the conversation and can be handed over".

**A claim that needs honest handling.** "Communicate to all models" is not something Skills
deliver — they are Anthropic's format and will not load into another vendor's product. Rather
than let him find this out at week six, L03 states it directly and reframes what genuinely
transfers: the written process in plain Markdown, portable across Claude surfaces and reusable
by hand anywhere. Recorded here because it is a misconception to correct early, and correcting
it will predict how he reasons about lock-in later.

**Also decided:** Claude Code is parked. He was shown "comfortable in the terminal" as an option
and deliberately wrote something else instead, so it is not his goal — and at under 30 min/week
it would consume two months better spent on Stages 01–03. Kept as an optional Stage 04 available
on request, not scheduled. See [MISSION.md](../MISSION.md).

**Status: awaiting his confirmation.** The revised mission is an interpretation of one sentence
he wrote. He should get to correct it before more lessons are built on it.
