# Working with Claude beyond chat — Glossary

The canonical vocabulary for this workspace. Lessons and learning records use these words
and no synonyms.

Per [GLOSSARY-FORMAT](/Users/arjun/.claude/skills/teach/GLOSSARY-FORMAT.md), a term is
promoted here only once the learner can *use* it correctly — not when it is first taught.
Below are the terms he already owns from chat use. Everything else waits for evidence.

## Terms

**Project**:
A saved workspace in the Claude app holding files and standing instructions that apply to
every conversation inside it.
_Avoid_: Workspace, folder, custom GPT

**Artifact**:
A self-contained document, page, or app Claude produces as an addressable thing rather than
as chat text.
_Avoid_: Canvas, output, side panel
