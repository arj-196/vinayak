### Track B · usage and context

Runs alongside the main path. A process that dies halfway through when your allowance runs out
isn't actually implemented, so these three exist to stop that happening.

| | Lesson | Time | What you walk away with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** | [Find your meter](lessons/0003-find-your-meter.html) | 8 min | You know your own reset times |
| **B2** | [What that actually cost](lessons/0004-what-that-cost.html) | 10 min | Cowork's price stops being a mystery |
| **B3** | [Making a task fit](lessons/0005-making-a-task-fit.html) | 12 min | You can size a job to the clock |

**Do them in this order:** L01 → L02 → **B1** → L03 → L04 → L05 → L06 → L07 → **B2** → **B3** → L08 → L09.

Two of those have timing that matters. **B1 is worth doing this week**, even before L02 — it takes
five minutes and captures a "before" reading that stops existing once you start using Cowork.
**B2 belongs on the same day as L07**, while the run it measures is still fresh in your head.

## Three things before you start

**Fifteen minutes is the ceiling, not the target.** Lessons are built to fit under 30 minutes a
week with room spare. If one runs long, stop mid-lesson and come back — none of them punish you
for it.

**Every lesson ends with something you made.** There is no lesson that is only reading. One screen
of explanation, then hands on keys.

**One thing is still missing from your answers.** You left "the task you'd most like to stop doing
by hand" blank. No problem — L01 makes you pick one anyway. Whatever you choose becomes the thread
running through L08 and the finish line, **so tell Arjun which one you picked.** That's the only
homework outside the lessons.
