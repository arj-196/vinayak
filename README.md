# Master processes with Claude

A short course, built for you. Nine lessons, none longer than fifteen minutes, ending with a
process a colleague can run without you explaining it.

You already get good results out of the chat box. This is about everything after it.

---

## Start here

**→ [L01 — Your first Skill, by talking](lessons/0001-your-first-skill.html)** · 15 min
You describe a process out loud, Claude writes the file. No file editing at all.
*You'll finish with a Skill that fires without you naming it.*

**→ [L02 — Why it didn't fire](lessons/0002-why-it-didnt-fire.html)** · 12 min
One field decides whether Claude ever reaches for your Skill. You break yours on purpose, then fix it.
*You'll finish able to debug a Skill that stays silent.*

Those two are written and ready. Do L01 this week and stop there — the rest arrive as you reach them.

## Three things before you start

**Fifteen minutes is the ceiling, not the target.** Lessons are built to fit under 30 minutes a
week with room spare. If one is running long, stop mid-lesson and come back — none of them
punish you for it.

**Every lesson ends with something you made.** There is no lesson that is only reading. One
screen of explanation, then hands on keys.

**One thing is still missing from your answers.** You left "the task you'd most like to stop
doing by hand" blank. No problem — L01 makes you pick one anyway. Whatever you choose there
becomes the thread running through L08 and the finish line, **so tell Arjun which one you
picked.** That's the only homework outside the lessons.
