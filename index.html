Rulesthree of them

Three things before you start

  • Fifteen minutes is the ceiling, not the target. Lessons are built to fit under 30 minutes a week with room spare. If one runs long, stop mid-lesson and come back — none of them punish you for it.
  • Every lesson ends with something you made. There is no lesson that is only reading. One screen of explanation, then hands on keys.
  • Nothing here needs installing. Every lesson is a web page. Click a link, read, do the thing in your Claude desktop app. That is the whole setup.
